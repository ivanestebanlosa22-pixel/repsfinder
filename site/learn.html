Follow these 6 steps to make your first purchase successfully

2

Choose your purchase agent

Compare verified agents and choose the one that best fits your needs.

  • Compare commissions between agents
  • Check shipping times to your country
  • Verify they have support
  • Use bonus codes for discounts
3

Register with bonus code

Use our exclusive codes to get registration bonuses and discounts.

  • Copy the code of the chosen agent
  • Register with the provided link
  • The bonus will apply automatically
  • Save the code for future purchases
4

Place your order

Copy the product link and paste it in your agent.

  • Copy the product link
  • Paste it in the agent's website
  • Select size and color
  • Confirm the order
5

Request QC photos

Before shipping, request quality control photos to verify the product.

  • Wait for it to arrive at the warehouse
  • Request detailed QC photos
  • Check logos, seams and details
  • Compare with official photos
  • Approve or reject the product
⚠️ Important: Never approve shipping without seeing QC photos first. It's your quality guarantee.
6

Receive your order

Track your shipment and receive your package at home.

  • Save the tracking number
  • Track with 17track.net
  • Receive the package at your address
  • Verify contents upon receipt
